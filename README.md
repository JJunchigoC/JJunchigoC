### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an incoming **PhD Student at North Carolina State University** (Fall 2026). Currently, I am finishing my Master's at **Civil Aviation University of China**, focusing on **Additive Manufacturing** and **Intelligent Control Systems**.

My research combines **Engineering** with **Computer Science**, applying Machine Learning (YOLOv8) and optimization algorithms to solve real-world manufacturing problems.

* 🎓 **Education:**
    * **PhD** (Incoming): North Carolina State University 🇺🇸
    * **M.S.** in Transportation Engineering: Civil Aviation University of China (2022-2025) 🇨🇳
    * **B.S.** in Electrical Engineering: Nanjing University of Technology Pujiang Institute (2018-2022) 🇨🇳

* 🔭 **Research Interests:**
    * **Smart Manufacturing:** High-speed 3D printing & Defect Detection.
    * **Algorithms:** PID Control, Ant Lion Optimization, & Deep Learning.
    * **Medical AI:** Depression prediction using Speech & Scales (Research Assistant at NJMU).
